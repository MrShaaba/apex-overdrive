# Asset attribution

All car and cockpit models are CC-BY-4.0. Attribution is required wherever this
project is shared. Raw downloads live in the gitignored `assets-src/` folder; the
files under `public/assets/` are compressed derivatives of them.

## nqweba_dawn (sky panorama)

"Nqweba Dawn" HDRI from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com/a/nqweba_dawn), licensed CC0 1.0
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Converted from the 4k EXR to a
2k Radiance .hdr by tools/asset-pipeline/convert-hdri.mjs.
